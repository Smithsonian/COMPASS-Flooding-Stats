Abstract

Biomass surveys conducted each fall from 2015 to 2018 at the Global Change Research Wetland (GCREW) marsh, Corn Island, Bear Neck Creek, and other tidal wetland sites located at the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center in Edgewater, Maryland. Biomass measurements include allometry and percent cover classes.
